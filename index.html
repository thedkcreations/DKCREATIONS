Shipping Policy

We handcraft every rangoli to order, so please allow 7–10 business days for your order to be prepared, packed, and shipped from Pune. Delivery times may vary slightly depending on your location and courier availability, especially during festive seasons when order volumes are high.

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation with tracking details on the email/phone number provided at checkout. We currently ship Pan-India. All orders are prepaid — Cash on Delivery (COD) is not available at this time.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

As each rangoli is handcrafted specifically for your order, we do not offer returns, refunds, or cancellations once an order has been placed and payment confirmed.

In the rare case your item arrives visibly damaged or defective in transit, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery at thedkcreations@gmail.com with photos of the damaged item and packaging, and we'll review it on a case-by-case basis.
